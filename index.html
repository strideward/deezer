Terms of Service

Last updated 15 November 2026.

Using the service

Use Parrallery lawfully. Keep your login secure. Don't impersonate, harass, manipulate plays, disrupt the service, or bypass restrictions.

Music and other content

Upload only content you own or can share. Artist verification requires exclusive rights to every upload and 2 original uploads; covers, remixes, and freestyles don't count. You keep ownership and let Parrallery store, process, display, and play your uploads. Illegal or infringing content may be removed.

Visibility and current limits

Profiles and songs may be public. Hide your profile in Settings where available. Songs are limited to 10 MB. Private releases, scheduling, checkout, and creator payouts aren't available.

Availability and changes

Features may change or stop. This summary isn't full legal terms; publish reviewed terms and contact details before launch.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026.

Information in your account

We store your email, username, and profile details (name, hometown, bio, genre, avatar, and links). Public details and songs may be visible to others. Don't share anything you want private.

Content and activity

We store songs, artwork, comments, messages, follows, likes, downloads, plays, and reports to run and protect the app. Verification confirmations and their submission time are stored too.

Service providers and device storage

Supabase stores accounts, data, and files. Text used for AI writing or report checks may go to Google Gemini. Preferences and cached audio stay in your browser; clear site data to remove them.

Your choices and deletion

Edit your profile in Settings. Deletion hides your profile first; permanent removal may take longer. Clear browser data to remove saved preferences and audio.
