Creator authentication

This eligibility checklist is based on Audiomack’s published creator-authentication requirements. Parrallery does not currently authenticate creators or offer AMP monetization.

On Audiomack, authentication confirms creator identity and content rights. It is required before applying for AMP and can unlock creator tools, fan notifications, trending submissions, and recommendation eligibility.

Eligibility requirements

Loading…

Content rights

You must be the creator or rights holder of your uploads. Music you do not own or control is not eligible for authentication.

Social account

Link and authenticate an X/Twitter or Instagram account. Social account authentication and application review are not available in Parrallery yet. Meeting the numeric thresholds alone does not authenticate your account.

Audiomack earnings example

This section describes Audiomack's published programs only. These rates, thresholds, and payout terms are not Parrallery features, rates, or promises of earnings. Check Audiomack's current terms before relying on them.

AMP streaming royalties

Audiomack describes a monthly revenue pool based on ad and subscription revenue after actual costs. Revenue per stream varies by month and listener location; the creator share is 50% of AMP revenue share. There is no fixed per-stream rate. A stream must reach 30 seconds to qualify.

Supporters

Audiomack says creators receive 85% after app-store and payment-processing fees. If a distributor is involved, Supporters earnings may be paid to the distributor.

Illustrative AMP calculation: monetized streams × that month's revenue per stream × 50%. This is not a forecast or a Parrallery earnings calculation.

Audiomack AMP requirements

Creator accountAuthentication is separate from AMP enrollment and is required before applying.
Country and rightsBe based in an eligible country and own, license, or otherwise control the content rights.
Activate each uploadEligible uploads must be activated for monetization. Provide ISRC, ISWC, and UPC metadata where applicable.

Audiomack payouts and cautions

Timing and minimumAudiomack describes net-90 payouts and a $100 minimum streaming-royalty payout threshold; smaller balances carry forward. After funding, approval can take up to 4 business days, followed by 2–3 business days for the payment provider.
Keep activity authenticDo not buy streams, followers, or verification. Artificial activity can put eligibility and monetization at risk.
Program availabilityAudiomack describes AMP as being in advanced beta, with broader rollout planned for 2027. Availability and terms can change.

Terms of Service

Plain-language terms for using Parrallery. Last updated 14 November 2026.

Using the service

Use Parrallery lawfully and follow these terms. You are responsible for your account, keeping your sign-in details secure, and activity performed through it. Do not impersonate others, harass people, disrupt the service, manipulate plays or engagement, or bypass account restrictions.

Music and other content

You must own or have permission to upload and share every song, recording, image, and other file you submit. You keep ownership of your content. To operate Parrallery, you allow the service to store, process, display, and play content you upload or share according to your selected profile and release settings. Do not upload infringing, unlawful, or harmful material. Content may be hidden or removed when required by law or to enforce these rules.

Visibility and current limits

Profiles and songs may be public and discoverable. Use the profile visibility setting to hide your profile where available. Audio uploads are currently limited to 10 MB per song. Private releases, scheduled releases, song checkout, and creator payouts are not currently supported; do not rely on them being available.

Availability and changes

Parrallery is provided as available and features may change, be interrupted, or be removed. These terms are an in-app service summary and may not cover every legal requirement. Privacy Policy

A plain-language summary of information handled by Parrallery. Last updated 14 November 2026.

Information in your account

The app stores account details such as your email and username, and profile information you provide, including your name, hometown, biography, genre, avatar, and social links. Hometown, bio, links, and songs can be visible to others when your profile or content is public. Do not add information you do not want to share publicly.

Content and activity

Songs, artwork, comments, messages, follows, likes, downloads, and play counts may be stored to provide app features, show activity, and help protect the service. In-app messages are stored so conversations can work. Report details may be processed to review reported accounts or content.

Service providers and device storage

Parrallery currently uses Supabase for account authentication, database, and file storage. When you use AI-assisted description writing or report analysis, the relevant text may be sent to Google's Gemini API to provide that feature. Preferences are stored in your browser. The app can also cache song audio in browser Cache Storage for playback; this cache is limited by the app and can be removed by clearing this site's browser data.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit your profile and preferences in Settings. You can request account deletion there; a request hides the profile while it is pending, but permanent deletion may require a separate server cleanup and is not immediate. You can also clear browser-stored preferences and cached audio by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Questions
